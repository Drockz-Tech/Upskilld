Frequently Asked Questions

Is this program for beginners?

Yes! Our programs are designed for learners at all levels. We start with fundamentals and gradually build up to advanced concepts.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied after the first week, we'll provide a full refund.

Do I get recordings of the classes?

Yes, all live classes are recorded and available for you to revisit anytime during your course duration.

How much time do I need to dedicate per week?

We recommend 8-10 hours per week for optimal learning, but our flexible schedule allows you to learn at your own pace.
